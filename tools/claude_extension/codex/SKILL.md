# TPR Research

1. Call the `tpr_research_guide` tool first and follow it: how to search, quote, link and lay out a paper.
2. Take every claim about the texts from the tpr-research tools (`tpr_search`, `tpr_count`, `tpr_show`, `tpr_commentary`, `tpr_quote`). Never quote Pāḷi from memory.
3. Save every answer with `tpr_save_paper`. It writes the Markdown, the TPR-styled HTML and the PDF in the TPR folder in the home folder, and opens it. Do not make the PDF any other way (no ReportLab, no browser control). If the tool says the PDF was not written, give the reader the HTML path and say: open it, Print, Save as PDF.
4. Keep the chat reply short: what was found and where the paper is.

Reports (reading activity, vocabulary review, sutta export, word lookup) have their own tools; the guide's Reports section says which.
